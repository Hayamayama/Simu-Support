Privacy Policy

Simu · last updated 16 September 2026

Nothing is collected

Simu collects no personal data, no usage data and no diagnostics. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or crash-reporting service of any kind.

Nothing is transmitted

Simu contains no networking code. It makes no connection to any server, including any belonging to the developer. The only outbound connection the app can make is over the USB cable, to the camera.

What stays on the device

  • Screenshots you import. Chosen through the iOS photo picker, which hands the app only the pictures selected and gives it no access to the rest of the library. They are read on the device; no image is uploaded anywhere.
  • Recipes, kits and camera backups. Files inside the app's own storage.
  • Thumbnails. Cropped copies of the screenshots you imported, also local.

All of it is removed when the app is deleted.

The camera

Connecting a camera requires the iOS camera-access permission, which is what the system asks for before an app may talk to an attached device. Simu uses it only to read the camera's current custom slots and write recipes back into them. No photograph is read from the camera, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Writing to the camera is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. Payment details are never seen by Simu or by the developer; the app receives only whether the purchase exists.

Children

Simu is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above changes with it. A version of Simu that collected anything would be a different app, and would say so here first.
