Simu

Film recipes, read from a screenshot and written into the camera.

Contact

Email developer@bykrisyeh.com. One person answers it, usually within a couple of days.

A screenshot Simu read badly is the single most useful thing to send. The recipes people post are written in wording no dictionary covers completely, and every screenshot that arrives becomes a test case.

Setting up the camera

Two settings, both in the camera's own menus, and both have to be right before the cable does anything:

  • CONNECTION MODE → USB RAW CONV./BACKUP RESTORE
  • USB POWER SUPPLY/COMM SETTING → POWER SUPPLY OFF/COMM ON

The cable has to carry data. Charge-only USB-C cables are common and look identical.

Cameras

Writing is verified on hardware on the X100VI.

These fifteen bodies are supported, the rest from the camera's own firmware tables rather than from testing, and Simu says so before it writes: X100VI, X100V, X-T5, X-T50, X-T4, X-T3, X-T30, X-T30 II, X-E5, X-E4, X-M5, X-H2, X-H2S, X-S20, X-S10.

Every slot is copied before it is overwritten, on every camera. A write that goes wrong can be put back from inside the app.

Reports from a body other than the X100VI are welcome either way — the ones that work are as useful to hear about as the ones that do not.

What costs money

Reading screenshots, organising recipes into kits and the settings reference are free. Writing to the camera is a one-time purchase, restorable on any device signed into the same Apple ID.

Restoring a purchase

Tap a write control, then Restore Purchase on the screen that appears. The purchase belongs to the Apple ID it was made with; a different Apple ID is a different purchase.
